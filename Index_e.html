Samsung: Where Technology Meets Creativity

Explore the history of Samsung and dive into a journey marked by innovation, ethics, and a commitment to solid values. Discover how the company has transformed ideas into world-impacting solutions and learn about the mission that guides every step of its path.

Values

Samsung's values act as the moral and strategic compass that guides every decision and action of the company. They are the foundation upon which the mission is built, ensuring the company operates with integrity, excellence, and responsibility.

Values


People: Samsung's growth and success are directly linked to the talent and dedication of its employees. The company invests in its staff's development, fostering an environment where creativity and passion can flourish.


Excellence: Not settling for the good, but striving for the best. Samsung pursues excellence in everything it does, from research and development to the final quality of products and services, always with a focus on total customer satisfaction.


Change: The world is in constant evolution, and Samsung is always ahead. The company quickly adapts to new technologies and trends, continuously innovating to remain relevant and competitive.


Integrity: Acting with honesty and transparency is fundamental. Samsung operates according to the highest ethical and legal standards, building trust and respect with customers, partners, and society.


Mutual Prosperity: Believing in growth together. Samsung seeks to create value not only for itself but also for its partners, suppliers, and communities in general, contributing to a stronger and more prosperous ecosystem.


Mission

Samsung has a clear mission: to use its technology and talents to create excellent products and services that inspire the world. The goal is to enrich people's lives and contribute to social prosperity, transforming innovative ideas into experiences that truly make a difference. It's the philosophy of dedicating the best of the company to building a better future for global society.

The Approach: Growth and Contribution


To achieve its mission, Samsung adopts an approach based on three main pillars:

Focus on People and Technology: The company recognizes that its greatest assets are its employees and technological capability. Samsung invests in developing its talents and constantly seeks innovation, ensuring that knowledge and technology work in synergy.


Pursuit of Excellence: Samsung aims to create the best products and services in the market. This is achieved through a continuous commitment to quality, allowing it to maintain a global leadership position.


Social Commitment: The company understands that its role goes beyond profits. It strives to be a responsible corporate citizen, contributing to the well-being of communities and acting with integrity and transparency in all its operations.


Five Business Principles

Samsung is based on five business principles, which serve as the foundation of its Global Code of Conduct. They guide the company in its compliance with legal and ethical standards, in addition to defining its social responsibilities.

The five principles are:


Compliance with Laws and Ethical Standards: The company commits to respecting laws, human dignity, and diversity, competing fairly, and maintaining transparency in its financial transactions.


Maintaining a Clean Organizational Culture: Samsung seeks a work environment that promotes integrity and honesty, ensuring that employees feel safe to do the right thing.


Respect for Customers, Shareholders, and Employees: The company values open communication with its customers, seeks to increase value for its shareholders, and treats its employees with respect and fairness.


Care for the Environment, Health, and Safety: Samsung is dedicated to sustainable business practices and to ensuring a safe and healthy work environment for all.


Being a Socially Responsible Corporate Citizen: The company strives to contribute positively to the communities where it operates, fulfilling its tax obligations and supporting local development.


Timeline


1938

Foundation

Lee Byung-chul founded Samsung in Daegu, Korea, as a small trading company for local food, textiles, and exports.

1950–1960

Diversification of Businesses

Samsung expands into areas like textiles, insurance, media, and food, paving the way for a future entry into more technological industries.

1969

Samsung Electronics is Born

The creation of Samsung Electronics marks the beginning of the company's journey in the electronics sector, starting with the first black-and-white televisions.

1970–1979

First Electronic Products

Production of televisions, refrigerators, washing machines, and air conditioners. Samsung also begins investing in semiconductors.

1980

Entry into Telecommunications

Samsung launches its first telecommunications equipment and expands semiconductor production, establishing itself as a technology supplier.

1993

New Management

Under Lee Kun-hee's leadership, Samsung adopts the "Global Vision" focusing on quality and innovation, preparing to compete with international giants.

1997–1999

International Expansion

Despite the Asian crisis, Samsung invests in semiconductors, memory chips, and consumer electronics, becoming one of the leading global suppliers.

2000–2009

The Innovation Era

Launch of mobile phones, LCD televisions, and cutting-edge displays. Samsung consolidates its position as a global leader in smartphones and digital technology.

2010–2019

Leadership in Smartphones

The Galaxy line redefines the mobile market. Samsung invests in AMOLED displays, advanced chips, and becomes a reference in innovation in the tech sector.

2020–Present

Connecting the Future

Samsung strengthens its leadership in 5G, artificial intelligence, Internet of Things, and sustainability, guided by ethical values, innovation, and social impact.
