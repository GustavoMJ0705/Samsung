Samsung Ethics and Commitment

Explore our pillars of diversity, sustainability, and governance. Discover how our commitment to ethics shapes every innovation and strengthens our relationship with society.

Social Impact and Sustainability

Samsung understands that economic growth must be aligned with social and environmental responsibility. Therefore, it adopts sustainability practices in all phases of the value chain—from design to responsible product disposal.

Key environmental initiatives:
  • Energy efficiency: development of devices that consume less energy and reduce carbon emissions.
  • Circular economy: recycling and material reuse programs to extend the product lifecycle.
  • Operations management: factories and distribution centers focused on reducing environmental impact.
  • Sustainability reports: transparency in communicating goals and environmental results.
Our commitment goes beyond technological innovation: we work to protect the planet, promoting a circular economy, energy efficiency, and responsibility throughout our entire value chain.
Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion

Diversity and inclusion are core values in Samsung's culture. The company adopts policies that ensure fair work environments where everyone has equal opportunities and respect for human dignity.

Principles and actions for inclusion:
  • Non-discrimination environments: promoting respect for differences in gender, race, ethnicity, religion, sexual orientation, and gender identity.
  • Gender equity: encouraging female leadership in all areas of operation.
  • Global inclusion: valuing professionals from different nationalities, cultures, and experiences.
  • Training and opportunities: training and development programs aimed at including minority groups.
At Samsung, we believe that innovation is born from diversity. Our commitment is to build an inclusive environment where every person is respected, valued, and has the chance to grow.
Ethics and Internal Policies

Samsung's governance is supported by a global code of conduct and the Five Business Principles, which guide the company's actions in all markets.

Samsung's Five Business Principles:
  • Comply with laws and ethical standards.
  • Respect human rights and value people.
  • Compete fairly.
  • Maintain transparent accounting.
  • Act as a responsible corporate citizen.
To ensure the effective practice of these principles, Samsung maintains:
  • Continuous ethics training for employees.
  • Compliance programs and accessible reporting channels.
  • Internal and external audits to ensure transparency.
  • Global corporate responsibility, aiming to contribute to the development of the communities where it operates.
Business Strategy

Samsung's strategy combines technological leadership and constant investments in research and development (R&D) with a long-term vision focused on sustainability, ethics, and inclusion. The company operates in an integrated manner, offering complete solutions that unite hardware, software, and services, allowing organizations to improve their efficiency, security, and innovation capacity.

Strategic Pillars:
  • Continuous innovation: launching products and solutions that anticipate trends and meet emerging market demands.
  • Sector focus: customization of solutions for specific areas such as health, education, retail, finance, and transportation.
  • Co-creation and partnerships: collaboration with companies, governments, and startups to accelerate digital transformation.
  • Sustainable leadership: integrating economic growth with social impact and respect for the environment.
We don't just deliver products: we offer complete innovation ecosystems that help companies and people thrive in a connected and sustainable world.
